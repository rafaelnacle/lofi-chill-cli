## Fireplace

- Work: [Fireplace Sound loop](https://opengameart.org/content/fireplace-sound-loop)
- Author: PagDev
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode)
- Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/fire.wav
- Included file: `fire.mp3` (approximately 28.26 seconds).

Both additions use the original ambient audio, normalized with FFmpeg loudnorm (I=-24, TP=-3, LRA=7). The final second is crossfaded into the first second using linear curves; the loop starts at the original second 1. Encoded as stereo, 44.1 kHz MP3 at 160 kbps. Attribution is retained for provenance; no external audio service is contacted during playback.
